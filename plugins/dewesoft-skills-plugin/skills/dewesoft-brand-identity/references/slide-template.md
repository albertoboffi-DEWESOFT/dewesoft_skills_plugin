# Slide Template (.pptx)

The Dewesoft deck look is derived from the **openDAQ** presentation. Reproduce this **design**; the
content changes per deck. Read together with `visual-identity.md` and
`/mnt/skills/public/pptx/SKILL.md`.

Canvas: 16:9. Foundation is **black** (`#000000`) or DS Black (`#232323`) backgrounds with **white**
text and **DS Orange** accents. High contrast, generous negative space, bold titles.

---

## 1. Master / every slide

- **Background:** black `#000000` (or `#232323`).
- **Logo:** Dewesoft **horizontal logo, Type B** (orange mark + white `DEWESoft®`) in the **top-right
  corner**, small. On co-branded decks, place `DEWESoft®` + partner logo together (top-left of title
  slides), keeping clear space.
- Body text white `#FFFFFF`; secondary text DS Silver `#A7A7A7`; emphasis DS Orange `#F15D2D`.

---

## 2. Title / cover slide

- Black background with a **glowing orange waveform / oscilloscope-trace** graphic on the right side
  (the signature hero visual). Faint orange crosshair gridlines may cross it.
- Co-brand lockup top-left (`DEWESoft®` [+ partner]).
- **Title:** very large **white bold**, left-aligned (e.g. *openDAQ – Why and How?*).
- **Subtitle:** large white/lighter regular beneath (e.g. *Extending Dewesoft ecosystem beyond the box*).
- **Presenter block** lower-left: name in **white bold** (e.g. `Alberto Boffi`), role in DS Silver
  below (e.g. `Aerospace, Defence and Integration Engineer`).

The same hero layout is reused for the closing slide (e.g. *Let's build something together / Questions?*).

---

## 3. Content slide titles — the signature pattern

Top-left, large. **First keyword in DS Orange bold, remainder in white bold**, separated by ` - `:

- `DEWESOFT X` *(orange)* ` - ` `Where it excels` *(white)*
- `OPENDAQ` *(orange)* ` - ` `What is it?` *(white)*
- `NVH` *(orange)* ` - ` `Your world, your rules` *(white)*

Always lead the title with the orange keyword. This is the strongest recurring brand cue of the deck.

---

## 4. Section-divider slide

- Black background, a **product/hardware photo on the left** with dramatic spotlight lighting.
- **Big DS Orange bold title** on the right (e.g. `DEWESOFT X`, `OPENDAQ`).
- One white subtitle line beneath the title.

---

## 5. Card & column layouts

**3-column feature cards:** each card = dark panel (`#1A1A1A`/`#232323`) with a **colored accent bar
across the top**, an icon in the accent color, a **white card title**, and light-grey/white body text.

**2-column "challenge → solution":** left cards carry a **DS Orange** left-accent border (the
problem); right cards a **green** left-accent border (the solution); **white chevron arrows** point
left→right between them. The active row gets a full colored outline (orange left / green right).
Within copy, highlight problem words in orange and benefit words in green.

**Cascade / architecture list:** stacked full-width bars stepping down-and-right, each with a
**left accent color bar**; bar title in the accent color, description in **Oswald Light + DS Silver**
(no italic).

### Secondary category palette (cards/accents only)

Beyond DS Orange, the deck codes categories with a small bright palette — used **only** as thin
accent bars, icons, and outlines on dark cards, never as large fills or text color in documents:

| Accent | HEX |
|---|---|
| Orange (primary) | `#F15D2D` |
| Lime / yellow-green | `#C6F032` |
| Cyan / light blue | `#7FD4E0` |
| Teal | `#1F9BB8` |
| Green | `#4FB477` |
| Purple | `#9B59FF` |

Keep black + white + orange dominant; the extra accents are seasoning, not the scheme.

---

## 6. Typography on slides

- Titles: **Oswald Bold**, large (Oswald is condensed — strong for big titles).
- Body: **Oswald Regular / Arial fallback**, white.
- Descriptions in card/cascade layouts: **Oswald Light + DS Silver** (Oswald has no italic — do not
  fake italic).
- Selective inline emphasis: orange (focus/problem) or green (benefit); otherwise white.
- **Padding / clear space:** keep **≥ 16–20 px inner padding** inside cards and panels so the
  condensed text never crowds the accent bars, borders, or chevrons; keep clear space between an
  accent bar and the text it labels.

## Pre-delivery checklist

- [ ] Black background; Type B logo top-right on every slide.
- [ ] Title slide uses the orange-waveform hero + co-brand lockup + presenter block.
- [ ] Content titles follow **orange keyword + white remainder** with ` - ` separator.
- [ ] Cards on dark panels with colored top/side accent bars + icons.
- [ ] Challenge/solution uses orange-left / green-right with white chevrons.
- [ ] Black + white + orange dominate; secondary palette only as accents.
- [ ] **Oswald / Arial** throughout; no off-brand fonts; no faux italic (use Light + DS Silver).
- [ ] Card/panel inner padding ≥16–20 px; text never crowds accent bars, borders, or chevrons.
- [ ] No invented specs; figures/claims sourced.
